A calm clinic, built around how you actually feel in the chair

Larkspur Dental Studio was designed for people who put off the dentist. Soft lighting, quiet equipment and a team that explains each step before it happens make routine care feel straightforward.

Our philosophy is simple: prevent problems early, treat only what is needed, and never surprise you with a bill. Every treatment plan is written down, costed and discussed before we begin.

  • Three private treatment rooms and a separate children's area
  • Digital X-rays and intraoral cameras so you can see what we see
  • Noise-reducing headphones, blankets and numbing gel for anxious patients
  • Step-free access, with a quiet room for recovery after treatment

Treatments for every stage of your smile

From six-monthly check-ups to complete restorations, everything is carried out under one roof by a team that knows your history.

Cosmetic dentistry

Veneers, bonding and smile design planned around your face, your teeth and what you want to change. We show you a preview before anything is done.

Dental implants

A fixed, natural-feeling replacement for missing teeth, planned with 3D imaging and placed with a clear, staged timeline.

General dentistry

Check-ups, fillings and gum care, with problems caught early while they are small.

Teeth cleaning

Gentle scale and polish that removes plaque and staining, with simple advice for home care.

Teeth whitening

Dentist-supervised whitening in clinic or with custom trays at home, kept gentle on enamel and gums.

Braces and aligners

Clear aligners and traditional braces for teens and adults, with progress checks at every stage.

Root canal treatment

Pain relief and tooth preservation using magnification and local anaesthetic for a comfortable visit.

Crowns

Strong, natural-shade crowns that protect weakened teeth and restore how they look and work.

Pediatric dentistry

A relaxed first visit for little ones, then regular check-ups, fissure sealants and fluoride care. We let children explore the chair, the lights and the tools before anything is done, so dentistry stays familiar rather than frightening.

Questions patients ask us

Can't find your answer? Contact the clinic and we'll help.

Sample answers. Policies will be updated to match the clinic.

How do I book, change or cancel an appointment?

Use the booking form, call us or message us. To change or cancel, please let us know at least 24 hours ahead so another patient can take the slot.

What happens at a first consultation?

You'll meet your dentist, talk through your history and concerns, and have an examination with any X-rays that are needed. You leave with a clear explanation, options and written costs.

Does treatment hurt?

We use local anaesthetic, numbing gel and a slow, step-by-step approach. You can raise a hand to pause at any time. If you feel anxious, tell us and we will adapt your visit.

How often should I have a check-up and clean?

Most adults benefit from a check-up every six to twelve months. Your dentist will recommend an interval based on your gum health and cavity risk.

How do you keep the clinic hygienic?

Instruments are cleaned, sterilised and sealed before use, surfaces are disinfected between patients, and single-use items are never reused.

What payment options do you offer?

We accept cards and bank transfer, and can discuss instalment plans for larger treatments. We can also provide paperwork for your insurer. Costs are agreed in writing before work begins.
